### Hi, I'm Felix 👋

I'm a student who builds things. Right now that's **[VYRSE](https://github.com/felixthosharon/vyrse)**: a JARVIS-style AI assistant that runs on your own PC.

Every AI I tried could talk, but none could actually use my computer. So I built one that can: it listens for its name, talks back in your style, controls your PC and phone, reads your screen, builds charts and quizzes as it answers, and keeps working with the internet off. Free and open source.
